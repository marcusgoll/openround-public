# Community conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming across
experience levels and backgrounds. Give specific, useful feedback and respect
other people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing
private information without consent are not acceptable. These expectations apply
to repository issues, pull requests, and project community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and warn, restrict, or ban
participants. Enforcement should consider impact and context and avoid exposing
private reports. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.

To report a concern, request a private contact channel from the maintainer without
posting sensitive details publicly. For urgent platform abuse, use GitHub's report
abuse tools. A decision may be appealed through the private channel with relevant
new context.
